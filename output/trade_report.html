How this is scored, and where it falls short
  • Stats come from Basketball-Reference season tables (via the public sumitrodatta/bball-reference-datasets mirror). Seasons are labeled by the year they end, so 2008 means 2007-08.
  • Value counts only what a player gave the team that traded for him, during his first unbroken stay there. Mid-season trades count only the post-trade stint. Players who were flipped or waived without playing show 0, which is the point.
  • Draft picks count when the player picked is famous enough to be part of the story (Marc Gasol, Steven Adams, Jaylen Brown, Jayson Tatum, Enes Freedom, Collin Sexton, Austin Rivers). Other picks aren't valued, so pick-heavy hauls like the Gobert and George deals are undercounted for the team that got the picks.
  • Long tenures win. A 23-year-old who stays ten years will out-score a 31-year-old who won you a title in two. That's a real cost of the method: Win Shares don't know what a ring is worth.
  • The franchise report card only covers these 57 trades, not every deal a team made. Recent deals (2025 and 2026) are still being written; their totals will grow.
  • Deadline movers include a handful of waiver claims and buyout signings, since the stat lines can't tell those apart from trades.
